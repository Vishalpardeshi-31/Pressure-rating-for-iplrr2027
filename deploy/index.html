How the rating is built and where it is uncertain

Rating = 0.6 × the conservative pressure record + 0.4 × usual level, each standardised across rated players of the same discipline. The conservative pressure record is the player's value in high-pressure balls, pulled towards average for small samples and lowered by 0.674 standard errors.

A player is rated with at least 120 high-pressure balls, 150 ordinary balls and 12 matches containing four or more pressure balls.

"Raises game" and "Drops" mean the gap between pressure and usual value is larger than one standard error. That gap was not repeatable between halves of the data, so it is shown as evidence and is not part of the rating.

The chance of staying in the top 10 comes from resampling each player's matches 1,000 times. Ranks 8 to 15 are close to interchangeable.

Rain-affected matches, no-results and two A-team tournaments are left out. Fielding, venue and pitch are not modelled.
